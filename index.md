Clarp is the iPhone and iPad app for coding agents that already run on a computer you own. Pair the app with a Host — that machine, with the Clarp server installed — and every session on it is a chat in your pocket.

These pages are the public legal and support documents for the **app**. They are what the App Store listing links to.
