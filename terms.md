## What Clarp is

Clarp is a client for a server you run yourself, on a computer you control (a "Host"). The app sends your instructions to the Host; software on the Host — including AI coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex that you have installed and signed into — carries them out. Clarp does not provide the AI models and does not run your code. Your relationship with those AI providers, including their pricing and acceptable-use rules, is between you and them.

- Keep your device and Host credentials safe. Pairing issues a per-device token; revoke it from the Host if a device is lost.
- Use Clarp only on systems you are authorised to access, and in compliance with applicable law.
